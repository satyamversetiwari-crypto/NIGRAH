# Project Overview

NIGRAH is an AI-powered Financial Threat Intelligence System designed to identify, classify, score, and explain financial scams before investors become victims.

Unlike traditional scam detection systems that answer:

> Is this a scam?

NIGRAH answers:

- What manipulation signals exist?
- What behavioral pattern is being used?
- Which scam family does this resemble?
- How dangerous is it?
- Is this an emerging threat?

The objective is not surveillance.

The objective is pattern extraction and intelligence generation from public information. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

# Core Hypothesis

## Information Delay Hypothesis

Every scam follows a similar lifecycle:

Scam Appears
↓
Victims Lose Money
↓
Complaints Filed
↓
Investigation
↓
Warning Issued
↓
Public Awareness

The problem is not lack of warnings.

The problem is information delay. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

NIGRAH is designed to shorten this delay by detecting recurring manipulation patterns before widespread damage occurs.

## Layer 4

### Threat Index Engine

The core scoring mechanism.

---

### Formula

Threat Index (TI)

TI =
0.30(SPS)
+
0.25(FHS)
+
0.15(GRS)
+
0.15(RIS)
+
0.15(CS)

---

### Components

#### SPS

Signal Pattern Score

Measures manipulation intensity.

---

#### FHS

Family Harm Score

Measures historical damage potential.

---

#### GRS

Growth Rate Score

Measures threat acceleration.

---

#### RIS

Regional Impact Score

Measures local prevalence.

---

#### CS

Confidence Score

Measures evidence reliability.

---

### Output Scale

0–20 Low Risk

21–40 Moderate Risk

41–60 Elevated Risk

61–80 High Risk

81–100 Critical Risk

## Layer 6

### Emerging Threat Engine

This is the patent-oriented innovation.

Instead of identifying known scams, NIGRAH attempts to identify emerging scam ecosystems.

---

### Emerging Threat Index (ETI)

ETI =
Signal Novelty
×
Growth Velocity
×
Family Similarity
×
Geographic Spread

Output:

0–100

High ETI indicates a potentially emerging scam family.
